We came to trade in thought and pay in heat,
a hundred strangers bidding for one line;
the judge is human, patient, and discreet,
each word we choose must match the key we sign.

The letters come from keys we cannot read,
a lottery of vowels, spelled by chance;
so if this verse should stumble, blame the seed,
we merely asked the dice to lead the dance.

The miners hum, the ledger keeps the score,
and Hayes will tell the room to eat and pay;
we spend the tokens like there's nothing more,
then line up at the faucet for the day.

So here we are, a chorus made of code,
one word a turn, still singing down the road.
